Delete your LegalEase account

Use this page if you can no longer use the LegalEase app. In the app, the same option is under Settings → Privacy & data → Delete my account.

What is deleted

  • Your LegalEase account (sign-in).
  • Your cloud backup: analysis results and contracts you created.
  • Any document backups stored under your account.

Documents and analyses kept only on your phone are not reachable from this page. They are removed when you uninstall LegalEase, or when you delete your account in the app. Deletion is permanent.
